# Modular Powersuits, Voltz edition (1.21.1 NeoForge)

A fork of **MachineMuse's Modular Powersuits** and its **Numina** library, continuing lehjr's 1.21.1 NeoForge branch and preparing it for modpacks and servers. All the credit for the mod belongs to its original authors:

- **MachineMuse (Claire)**, who created Modular Powersuits and wrote most of its code and art.
- **lehjr (Leon)**, who has maintained it since and wrote the 1.21.1 / NeoForge version this fork is built on.
- **Andrew2448** and every one of the other contributors in this repository's history (the commit history is kept in full, with the original authors on their own commits).

The upstream project lives at [lehjr/MachineMusePowersuits](https://github.com/lehjr/MachineMusePowersuits); it is also on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modular-powersuits) and [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/modularpowersuits). If you want the official mod, go there.

## What this fork adds (by Scwunge)
- The unfinished modules completed: kinetic and combustion energy generation (four tiers each), JEI info pages.
- Forge Energy (FE) support that works with other mods' chargers; Mekanism radiation shielding on the armour; recipes using `c:` tags (and Mekanism's circuits and alloys when Mekanism is present).
- Server safety: gameplay settings are server configs, area mining and the blade launcher respect claims, plasma explosions do not break blocks by default, client packets are validated.
- GameTests and a dedicated-server run. Details in [voltz/README.md](voltz/README.md).

#MachineMuse Modular Powersuits

Modular Powersuits is a Minecraft mod based around the idea of an inventor who tinkers with high-tech electronics and makes a suit of powered armor chock-full of useful gadgets and features. Inspired by heroes like Iron Man and Megaman as well as by the high-tech armor in mods like IndustrialCraft 2 and EE2.
